What Inspect Does

Inspect is a modern certificate inspector for iOS. You can paste a host name or HTTPS URL, open it from the share sheet, review the server's trust chain, and export DER certificates when needed.

  • Inspect live HTTPS endpoints directly from iPhone or iPad.
  • Review X.509 fields, SANs, EKUs, AIA, SKI/AKI, policies, fingerprints, and public key details.
  • See trust evaluation status and security findings in plain language.
  • Export certificates for debugging or incident response.

Privacy Summary

Inspect does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

  • Recent lookups are stored locally on your device.
  • TLS inspection requests go only to the host you choose to inspect.
  • Favicons for recent items may be fetched from Google's favicon service.
